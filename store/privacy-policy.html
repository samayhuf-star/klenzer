Klenzer Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

The short version

Klenzer works entirely on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your photos, videos or any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

What Klenzer accesses

With your permission, Klenzer reads your photo and video library on-device to detect duplicate or near-identical photos, screenshots and large videos, and to measure their file sizes. It also reads your device’s total and free storage to show it on the home screen.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Scan results are kept in memory on your device and discarded when you close the app. Your photos and videos are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

Klenzer stores two small preferences locally: whether you have finished the intro, and the running total of space you have cleared. Deleting the app removes them.

Deleting photos

Klenzer only deletes items you select, and only after your phone’s own confirmation prompt. On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted, where you can restore them for up to 30 days; the space is freed once that album is emptied. On Android, items you confirm are deleted by the system and Klenzer cannot restore them.

Permissions

Photo and video library access is the only sensitive permission Klenzer requests. You can change or revoke it at any time in your device Settings; Klenzer will still open but cannot scan.

Children

Klenzer collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown here and in the store listing, with a new date.
