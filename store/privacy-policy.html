Klenzer Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version

Klenzer scans and cleans entirely on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your photos or videos. There is no account and no advertising. If you turn notifications on, a notification service receives a device push token so we can send them (see Notifications below).

What Klenzer accesses

With your permission, Klenzer reads your photo and video library on-device to detect duplicate or near-identical photos, screenshots, Live Photos and large videos, and to measure their file sizes. It also reads your device’s total and free storage to show it on the home screen.

What leaves your device

Your photos, videos and scan results never leave your device. Scan results are kept in memory and discarded when you close the app. The only data that leaves your device is the notification data described below, and only if you turn notifications on.

What is stored on your device

Klenzer stores a few small preferences locally: whether you have finished the intro, the running total of space you have cleared, whether you turned notifications on, and (on iPhone) how much space is still waiting in Recently Deleted. Klenzer also keeps temporary files such as image thumbnails, which you can clear from the home screen. Deleting the app removes all of this.

Deleting photos

Klenzer only deletes items you select, and only after your phone’s own confirmation prompt. On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted, where you can restore them for up to 30 days; the space is freed once that album is emptied. iOS does not let apps empty it, so Klenzer offers a shortcut to Photos for you to do it. On Android, items you confirm are deleted by the system and Klenzer cannot restore them.

Notifications

Notifications are off unless you turn them on. If you do, Klenzer does two things. First, your phone schedules a few reminder notifications by itself (at most five over about six weeks, restarted whenever you open the app). Second, Klenzer registers your device with OneSignal, the service we use to send and measure push notifications. OneSignal receives your device’s push token, a random identifier it creates for this install, your IP address, device details (model, operating system, language, time zone, country, mobile carrier and app version), when and how often the app is opened, and whether a notification was delivered or opened. We use this only to send Klenzer notifications and to see how many are delivered and opened. It is not linked to your name, email or photos, and it is not used for advertising or sold. Nothing is sent to OneSignal before you turn notifications on. Turning notifications off in Klenzer Settings unsubscribes the device and stops this; you can also email us to have the record deleted.

Permissions

Photo and video library access is the only sensitive permission Klenzer requires. Notification permission is optional and only requested if you choose notifications. You can change or revoke either at any time in your device Settings; Klenzer will still open but cannot scan without photo access.

Children

Klenzer is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown here and in the store listing, with a new date.
