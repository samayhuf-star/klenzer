**Description:**
Your camera roll fills up with duplicates, old screenshots, large videos and forgotten recordings. Ai Klenzer scans your library on your device, shows you exactly what it finds, and lets you clear what you choose.

**WHAT AI KLENZER DOES**
• Screen recordings: find screen recordings so you can free space quickly.
• Large videos: surface videos over 100 MB so you reclaim the most space with the fewest taps.
• Similar and near-identical photos: groups look-alike photos and keeps the largest by default. You can change the pick.
• Screenshots: lists every screenshot, biggest first.
• Live Photos: review photos that also store a short video clip, and convert one to a still on iPhone.
• Blurry and low-quality shots: surface photos that are less useful to keep.
• Swipe to Organize: review a month of photos, one swipe at a time.
• Merge duplicate contacts on this device. Contacts never leave the phone.
• Secret Album: encrypted photos behind Face ID or a PIN, stored only on the device.
• Separate progress for photos, screenshots, and videos while scanning, including photos that live in iCloud.
• A notification when the scan finishes, if you allow notifications.
• Real storage numbers: free and total space straight from the system.

**WHY IT’S DIFFERENT**
• Scanning and reviewing are free. Without Pro you can clear 10 items a day. Pro removes that limit.
• Yearly and monthly can start with a 7-day free trial when the App Store says you are eligible. Lifetime is a one-time unlock with no trial.
• Nothing is deleted automatically. You review every item and confirm.
• Fully on-device. Photos are never uploaded or analysed in the cloud.
• Clear progress while scanning. You can leave the app and we’ll notify you when it’s done.

**YOU STAY IN CONTROL**
Deleted items go to Recently Deleted on iOS so you can change your mind. On Android, the system asks you to confirm before anything is removed.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**
Your photos are never uploaded, shared or analysed in the cloud. There is no account, no tracking and no ads. Purchase status for Ai Klenzer Pro is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play on the device.

**GOOD TO KNOW**
Ai Klenzer cleans photos, videos and duplicate contacts. It does not clear other apps’ data or system files. Photos stored only in iCloud are measured from a small thumbnail when the full file is not on the device.

Questions? Contact us from Settings › Contact support.

**What's New (1.1.1):** Renamed to Ai Klenzer. Blue scanning screen with per-category progress. iCloud thumbnail analysis, a scan-complete notification, and a 7-day trial on monthly and yearly when the store says you are eligible.

**App Review notes:**
Ai Klenzer is a native app (Expo/React Native) that uses PhotoKit via expo-media-library to scan the user's library on-device. No login required; no demo account needed. To test, allow Photos access and tap Smart Clean. Deletion uses the system confirmation dialog (PHPhotoLibrary performChanges). No data is transmitted off device. Photo access purpose is stated in NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription. The app can optionally schedule a local (on-device) reminder and a local scan-complete notification; no push server or remote notifications are used. Subscriptions: com.krosz.klenzer.yearly and com.krosz.klenzer.monthly (7-day introductory free trial when the Apple ID is eligible). Lifetime: com.krosz.klenzer.lifetime. Prices come from StoreKit.

### App Privacy ("nutrition label") answers
No data is collected by the app for purchases. Apple or Google processes the payment under their policies. The app checks store entitlements on the device only; it does not collect a user ID, and photos stay on the device. No Device ID and no advertising attribution.

- Core use case: **"Gallery / storage cleaner. The app's core purpose is scanning the user's full media library to find duplicates, screenshots and large videos."**
- Explain why the photo picker is insufficient: the app must enumerate and measure the whole library and delete selected items; a one-time picker cannot do that.
- Provide a short screen recording (scan → review → delete confirmation). Have this ready before submitting; without approval the build is rejected.

## Version source
`eas.json` sets `cli.appVersionSource` to `remote`, and the production profile sets `autoIncrement`. The `expo.version` in `app.json` is 1.1.1 so local and source builds show the intended release. The version and build number that ship are the remote EAS values. Set the remote version to **1.1.1** before `eas build`. Do not reuse build 25 from `ios.buildNumber` in `app.json`; builds 26 and 27 already exist, and the next production build should auto-increment from the remote counter.
