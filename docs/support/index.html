Support

For help with Klenzer, email samayhuf@gmail.com. There is no phone support line.

How does Klenzer work?

Klenzer reads your photo and video library on the device to find duplicate or near-identical photos, screenshots, Live Photos and large videos, and to measure their file sizes. You review what it finds and choose what to delete. Nothing is deleted until you confirm your phone’s own prompt. Your photos are not uploaded.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no login.

What does it cost?

Klenzer is free. There is no daily file limit and no paywall for basic cleaning.

Why does Klenzer need photo access?

It has to read your library to find and measure items, and to delete the ones you confirm. You can allow full access or selected photos. Without photo access, the app will open but cannot scan. You can change this later in the device Settings.

What are cleanup reminders?

They are optional. If you turn them on, Klenzer schedules a few occasional reminder notifications (at most five over about six weeks, restarted whenever you open the app). They are created and delivered by your phone itself. No server is involved, nothing is sent to us, and no push token is collected. You can turn them off in Klenzer Settings or in the device Settings.

I deleted items on iPhone but the space did not come back.

On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app. Klenzer cannot empty that album. Open Photos and empty Recently Deleted to free the space. Klenzer can show how much is waiting and offer a shortcut to Photos.
