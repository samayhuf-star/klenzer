Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Ai Klenzer is made by Samay Vashsht. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects almost nothing.

What stays on your device

Your scan results, preferences, and progress are stored on your device. We do not run accounts and we cannot see this data.

What we access

With your permission, Ai Klenzer reads your photo and video library on-device to find duplicates, screenshots, large videos, recordings, and Live Photos. It also reads your device’s free and total storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, shared, or analysed in the cloud.

Deleting

Nothing is deleted automatically. You review every item and confirm. On iOS, deleted items go to Recently Deleted. On Android, the system asks you to confirm.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics trackers, no sale of data, and no collection of contact lists, location, or photos beyond the on-device scan you start.

Children

Ai Klenzer does not knowingly collect information from children.
