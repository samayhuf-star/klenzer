Phone storage cleaner that stays on your phone.

Ai Klenzer scans your library on your device, shows you exactly what it finds, and lets you clear what you choose. Photos and videos are never uploaded.

Privacy policy Support

What it finds

Duplicate and near-identical photos, screenshots, Live Photos and large videos over 100 MB.

You stay in control

Nothing is deleted automatically. You review every item, then confirm with your phone’s own prompt.

Free forever

No account, no daily file limit and no paywall for basic cleaning. No ads and no tracking.

On this device

Scan results stay in memory on your phone and are discarded when you close the app.
