The app

Klenzer works entirely on your device. It finds duplicate or near-identical photos, screenshots, Live Photos and large videos, and lets you clear what you choose. It does not clear other apps’ data or system files. Photos stored only in iCloud may need to be downloaded before they can be reviewed.

Nothing is deleted automatically. You review every item and confirm. You are responsible for the items you choose to delete.

Klenzer only deletes items you select, and only after your phone’s own confirmation prompt. On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted, where you can restore them for up to 30 days; the space is freed once that album is emptied. iOS does not let apps empty it, so Klenzer offers a shortcut to Photos for you to do it. On Android, items you confirm are deleted by the system and Klenzer cannot restore them.

Klenzer is free. There is no account, no daily file limit and no paywall for basic cleaning. There is no analytics and no advertising.
