NSContactsUsageDescription => Klenzer finds and merges duplicate contacts on your device. Contacts never leave your phone.

NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription => Klenzer saves a still photo when you convert a Live Photo, and saves a photo back to your library when you export it from Secret Album. Nothing is uploaded.
NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription => Klenzer reads your photo library on-device to find duplicates, screenshots, Live Photos and large videos, to convert a Live Photo to a still when you ask, and to move photos you pick into Secret Album. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is deleted until you confirm.
