Support

For help with Ai Klenzer, email samayhuf@gmail.com. There is no phone support line.

How does Ai Klenzer work?

It scans your photo and video library on the device to find duplicates, similar photos, blurry photos, screenshots, Live Photos, recordings and large videos. You review what it finds and choose what to delete. Nothing is deleted until you confirm the system prompt. Your photos are not uploaded.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no login.

What is free, and what is Pro?

Scanning and reviewing are free. Without Pro you get 50 free items each day, shared across deleting photos and videos, merging contacts and adding photos to Secret Album. The count resets at local midnight and stays on your device. Ai Klenzer Pro removes that limit and adds Convert Live Photo to still. Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock (com.krosz.klenzer.lifetime) or an auto-renewing yearly (com.krosz.klenzer.yearly) or monthly (com.krosz.klenzer.monthly) subscription. Yearly and monthly show a 7-day free trial when the store says your account is eligible. Prices come from the store.

How do I restore or cancel a purchase?

On the Pro screen or in Settings, tap Restore purchase or Restore purchases. That checks your Apple ID or Google account for an existing purchase and unlocks Pro again if one is found. It does not charge you again. On iPhone, cancel a subscription in Settings under your Apple ID subscriptions. After Pro is unlocked, Manage subscription in the app opens that screen. On Android, subscriptions are managed in Google Play.

Why does Ai Klenzer need photo access?

It has to read your library to find and measure items, and to delete the ones you confirm. You can allow full access or selected photos. Without photo access the app still opens, but it cannot scan.

What are cleanup reminders?

They are optional. If you turn them on, the app schedules a local reminder every 3 days with your current storage, and replaces it with fresh numbers whenever you open the app. A scan can also send a local notification when it finishes, if you allowed notifications. No push server is used.

I deleted items on iPhone but the space did not come back.

On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos. Ai Klenzer cannot empty that album. Open Photos, then Albums, Recently Deleted, and delete the items there to free the space.

How does Merge Contacts work?

Open Contacts on the home screen and allow access. Ai Klenzer groups contacts that share a name, phone number or email. You preview the result, then merge. Missing phone numbers and email addresses are copied onto the contact that stays. Other details on the removed contacts are not copied. Contacts never leave your phone.

What is Secret Album?

It is an encrypted album for photos, stored only inside Ai Klenzer on your device, and unlocked with Face ID or a PIN. Photos in it are not uploaded. Ai Klenzer cannot recover a forgotten PIN, and deleting the app deletes Secret Album, so export anything you want to keep first.
