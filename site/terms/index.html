The app

Ai Klenzer is a phone storage cleaner. It finds duplicate and near-identical photos, blurry photos, screenshots, Live Photos, short recordings, screen recordings, and large videos on your device, and lets you delete the items you choose. It also offers Swipe to Organize, Merge Contacts, and Secret Album. It does not clear other apps’ data or system files, and it cannot empty Recently Deleted on iPhone for you.

Nothing is deleted automatically. You review items and confirm each deletion with the system prompt. You are responsible for the items you choose to delete. Photos and videos are processed on the device and are not uploaded.

Ai Klenzer Pro and in-app purchases

Scanning, reviewing, and browsing Swipe to Organize are free. Without Pro you get 50 free items each day, shared across deleting photos and videos, merging contacts, and adding photos to Secret Album. The count resets at local midnight. Unlimited use of those actions, and converting a Live Photo to a still, requires Ai Klenzer Pro. Pro is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details, and the app does not use RevenueCat.

  • a one-time lifetime unlock, product ID com.krosz.klenzer.lifetime, which does not renew.

The lifetime purchase has no trial.

Auto-renewing subscriptions

On iPhone, payment for a yearly or monthly subscription is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in Settings, under your Apple ID subscriptions. After Pro is unlocked, Ai Klenzer Settings also has Manage subscription, which opens that system screen. Apple’s subscription rules charge the renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends, so cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends if you do not want the next charge. The line on the paywall itself says that the plan renews until you cancel in Settings under your Apple ID subscriptions.

On Android, a subscription renews until you cancel it in your Google Play subscriptions. Manage subscription in the app opens that screen when Pro is unlocked. Refunds, if any, are handled by Apple or Google under their store rules.

Restore Purchases

If you reinstall Ai Klenzer or use another device, use Restore purchase on the Pro screen or Restore purchases in Settings. That asks the store whether this Apple ID or Google account already has a lifetime purchase or an active subscription. It does not charge you again for a purchase you already own. If the store has nothing to restore, the app says so and stays on the free allowance.
