Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026. Publisher: Samay Vashsht.

This policy describes how Ai Klenzer (“the app”) handles information. Ai Klenzer is a phone storage cleaner for iPhone and Android, published by Samay Vashsht.

The short version

Your photos, videos and contacts stay on your device and are never uploaded. There is no account and no sign-in. Ai Klenzer does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or RevenueCat. Purchases are completed on the device with the Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing. The app does not collect a name, email address, payment card, device identifier, or account ID.

Photos and videos

With your permission, Ai Klenzer reads your photo and video library on the device to find duplicate and near-identical photos, blurry photos, screenshots, Live Photos, short recordings, screen recordings, and large videos, and to measure their file sizes. It also reads the device’s total and free storage for the home screen. This media is processed on the device only and is never uploaded.

If a photo is stored only in iCloud, the scan can use a small thumbnail on the device to compare it. That thumbnail is not sent to us.

When you convert a Live Photo to a still, Ai Klenzer copies the original photo’s existing date, location, favourite state and album membership onto the new still. That location is metadata already saved in the photo. The app does not track where you are. The iOS configuration includes a location purpose string for that copy, so iOS may mention location when you convert.

Swipe to Organize shows your photos month by month so you can mark photos to keep or delete. Nothing is deleted until you confirm.

Ai Klenzer deletes or converts only the items you select, and only after your phone’s own confirmation prompt. On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted, where you can restore them for up to 30 days. On Android, items you confirm are deleted by the system and Ai Klenzer cannot restore them.

Contacts

Contacts access is optional and is requested only when you open Merge Contacts. If you allow it, Ai Klenzer reads the names, phone numbers and email addresses in your address book on the device to find likely duplicates. When you confirm a merge, it copies missing phone numbers and email addresses onto the contact that stays, then deletes the duplicate contacts from your address book. Other fields on the duplicates, such as addresses, birthdays, notes and photos, are not copied. Contacts are never uploaded or stored by Ai Klenzer outside your address book.

Secret Album and Face ID

Secret Album holds photos only. Photos you move into it are encrypted on the device with AES-256-GCM and stored only inside Ai Klenzer’s private storage on that device. They are not uploaded. The encryption key is created on your phone and kept in the system secure storage (the iOS Keychain, or the Android Keystore through the system secure-storage API), for this device only. Your PIN is never stored, only a salted hash of it, in that same storage.

Face ID is optional and is used only to unlock Secret Album. iOS checks your face and tells Ai Klenzer only whether it matched. Ai Klenzer never receives or stores face data. After a photo is added, Ai Klenzer can delete the original from Photos if you confirm. Ai Klenzer cannot recover a forgotten PIN, and deleting the app deletes Secret Album.

Free daily limit

Scanning and reviewing your library is free. Without Ai Klenzer Pro you get 50 free items each day, shared across deleting photos and videos, merging contacts and adding photos to Secret Album. The count resets at local midnight and stays on your device. Converting a Live Photo to a still has no free allowance; it requires Pro.

What is stored on your device

Ai Klenzer stores a few small preferences locally: whether you have finished the intro, how many free items you have used today, the running total of space you have cleared, whether you turned reminders on, and whether you chose not to see the Recently Deleted reminder again. It also keeps an on-device scan cache (file sizes and small image fingerprints, keyed by photo id and modification date) so later scans can skip unchanged items, plus temporary thumbnails you can clear in the app. None of this is uploaded. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, Ai Klenzer schedules a local notification every 3 days with your current used and total storage. Opening the app or bringing it back to the foreground cancels that reminder and schedules a fresh one. If you allow notifications when a scan starts, the app can also show a local notification when the scan finishes. Both are created on the phone. No server is involved, nothing is sent to us, and no push token is collected. You can turn reminders off in Ai Klenzer Settings.

Purchases

Beyond the free daily limit, deleting items, merging contacts and adding photos to Secret Album require Ai Klenzer Pro once the day’s 50 items are used. Converting a Live Photo to a still requires Pro. Pro is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play as an auto-renewing yearly subscription (com.krosz.klenzer.yearly), an auto-renewing monthly subscription (com.krosz.klenzer.monthly), or a one-time lifetime unlock (com.krosz.klenzer.lifetime). Prices are the store’s own localized price at the time of purchase. They are not hard-coded in the app.

Yearly and monthly show a 7-day free trial in the app only when the store reports that this Apple ID or Google account is eligible for the introductory offer. If the store does not report a trial, the app shows the regular price. The lifetime unlock has no trial.

The app talks to Apple or Google on the device, using StoreKit or Play Billing through the on-device purchase library, to load products, complete or restore a purchase, and check whether Pro is active. It does not use RevenueCat. We do not receive your payment card details, and purchase status is not sent to a server of ours. You can restore a previous purchase from Settings or from the Ai Klenzer Pro screen. After Pro is unlocked, Settings can open the system subscription manager.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Ai Klenzer has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK. It does not track you across apps or websites. It does not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or an account ID.

Permissions

Photo and video library access is needed to scan, delete, convert and add to Secret Album. Contacts access is optional and only requested when you open Merge Contacts. Face ID is optional and only used to unlock Secret Album. Notification permission is optional and only requested if you turn on reminders or when a scan starts. You can change or revoke any permission in your device Settings. The app still opens without photo access, but it cannot scan.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Your photos, videos and contacts are not shared with anyone. Apple or Google processes payments under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Everything Ai Klenzer stores, including Secret Album, stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Purchase records are held by Apple or Google. Restoring a purchase unlocks Pro again if the store still shows a valid purchase.

Children

Ai Klenzer is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. The same summary is shown in the app under Settings, Privacy Policy.
