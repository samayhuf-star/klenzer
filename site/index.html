Phone storage cleaner that stays on your phone.

Ai Klenzer scans your library on your device, shows you what it finds, and lets you clear what you choose. Photos and videos are never uploaded.

Privacy policy Support

What it finds

Duplicate and similar photos, blurry shots, screenshots, Live Photos, recordings and large videos.

You stay in control

Nothing is deleted automatically. You review every item, then confirm with your phone’s own prompt.

Free to scan

Scanning and reviewing are free. Without Pro you can clear 50 items a day. Pro removes that limit.

On this device

Photos, contacts and Secret Album stay on your phone. Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play.
